Glossary

Velmoran draws from two roots. A word's origin usually tells you which of the country's two founding peoples first used it. The exceptions are the interesting ones.

Velmar

Coast, lake, and highland
vel-
Kin; homeland. Velmora, Velgrad, Velmorsk, Velkomir. Its older form, val-, survives in Valdren.
val-
The people, meaning the assembled clans. An older form of vel-, kept only in the capital's name.
-dren
Hall; seat. Valdren — "seat of the people," the Hall where the clans met at midsummer.
hauk-
Hawk. Hauken — the national team's own name for itself, #HaukenXI.
veles
Falcon-kin; watcher. Radomir Veles.
-nes
Headland.
-vik / -vika
Bay; inlet.
-fell / -fjall
Mountain.
hall-ball
The clans' midsummer ball game, played at the moot with a stuffed leather ball and no fixed number of players. The reason football took so quickly.
saga
The Velmar way of keeping history: an argument polished until it sounds like a fact.

Few place names end in -nes, -vik or -fell today. Kovarai railway surveyors respelled most Velmar names by ear in the 1870s, and the maps kept their spelling.

Kovarai

Forge, mine, and rail
kovar-
Smith; forge. Root of Kovarai itself.
rudar
Miner. Rudar Braskov.
ruda
Ore. Braskov Ruda.
vatra
Fire, in the sense a movement keeps burning. FC Zoryn Vatra, founded in 1941 by resistance workers.
-ov / -ova
"Of," or "belonging to" — a place possessive. Braknov, Zarnov, Karnov.
-grad
Town; settlement. Velgrad — a Velmar root fused with a Kovarai suffix, marking a fusion settlement.
-sk
Place of. Velmorsk — another fusion, and a port the occupation held.
Spartak, Dynamo, Lokomotiv
Names borrowed from the wider labor movement: the slave who rebelled, the power station, the railway. Braknov Spartak, Kresna Dynamo, Sevrin Lokomotiv.

Both peoples

The words they share
-mir
Peace. The one word the two languages have in common. Velkomir, "great peace"; Radomir.

Ceremonial

Older than either people's everyday speech
korona
Crown. The bronze hawk-crown the Hall's speaker wore at moot, and the word the Crown Estate borrowed. Ostravaj Korona.
moot
The clans' midsummer assembly at the Hall at Valdren.
Warden
Keeper of the Hall, always of the Valdren family until the Charter. Lord Kaelan Valdren was the last.
lord / lady
A hereditary courtesy title held by a handful of old Velmar families since the republic's founding, carrying no constitutional standing.

Terms of the story

Events and institutions
The Line
The chain of cairns, customs posts, and gates the clans drew around the ore districts from about 1856. Abolished by the Charter.
The Collapse / the Uprising
The war of 1891, in the Velmar telling and the Kovarai telling. In Braknov, the Great Eastern Uprising.
The Charter
The Charter of Valdren, 1891: the republic's founding document.
The Crown Estate
The trust that holds the old clan families' lands. It still pays small patronage to a few clubs and institutions.
The Coast Occupation
1940 to 1945. Foreign troops held the ports; the Highlands and the interior were never occupied.
The Compact
The Reconstruction Compact of 1948: state direction of rail, power, and ore, until about 1990.
The Thaw
The end of the Compact, and the beginning of commercial football.
Northern Bay
Uncharted. Ships lose their compasses there.
Bellkeeper
One of the coast keepers who ring the fog bells on the north-east cliffs at dusk.
The Vanished XI
Velmoran football's name for the eleven who kept playing in 1891. Nobody knows more than that.
